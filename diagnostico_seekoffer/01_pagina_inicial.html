We make the transfer simple, supported and secure:

1. You buy the domain. Complete your purchase using any of our accepted payment methods. Your payment is held securely until the transfer is finished.

2. We send tailored transfer instructions. As soon as your payment clears, one of our transfer specialists emails you step-by-step instructions specific to the domain’s registrar and is on hand to help with anything technical.

4. The seller is paid. Only once the domain is safely in your possession do we release payment to the seller — so you’re protected throughout.

Once you’ve purchased, the domain is moved to you in one of two ways, depending on where it’s registered:

A registry is the organisation that operates a particular domain extension and maintains the master database of every domain under it. For example, Verisign operates the registry for .com. A registrar is an accredited company (such as GoDaddy, Namecheap or Cloudflare) that sells and manages domain registrations on the registry’s behalf, and is where you hold and control your domain day to day. In short: the registry runs the extension; the registrar is who you register and manage your domain with. When you buy a domain from us, it is transferred into your account at a registrar of your choice.
